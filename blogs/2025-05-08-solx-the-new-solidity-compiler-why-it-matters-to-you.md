For Solidity Developers - Write Clean Code, Spend Less Gassolx is a new optimizing compiler for Ethereum smart-contracts that could change the way you write and optimize Solidity code. Built on LLVM, solx focuses on improving runtime gas efficiency while reducing the need for manual workarounds. If you're a smart-contracts developer and gas costs matter to you, solx could help - some low-level tweaks you’re used to making might already be unnecessary, and more improvements are on the way...
