solx 0.1.2 targets binary size — cutting deployment costs and fitting more contracts under Ethereum’s 24 kB limit — without sacrificing gas efficiency or compile time. That focus turns into:Smaller binaries without losing gas efficiency. Contracts compiled with --via-ir are 13% smaller on median vs 0.1.1, and about 22% smaller than solc 0.8.30 –via-ir.More often under the 24 KB limit.
