The story of ZKsync has always been about scalability, decentralization, and building a better way forward for Web3. With the expansion of the Elastic Network, governance shifts, and a new era of interconnected ZK Chains, the community plays a greater role than ever. This evolution brings new opportunities for contributors to engage, shape discussions, and drive ecosystem growth.
