Incident SummaryOn April 13th, a compromised admin account minted the remaining unclaimed tokens from the ZK token Merkle distributors used for the ZKsync June 17th 2024 airdrop. The hacker successfully took control of 111,881,122 ZK tokens (equivalent to ~$5M at the moment of the initial transaction). The transaction can be viewed here.
